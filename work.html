How I use AI

New AI tools arrive every month. The value isn’t in trying them all. It’s in knowing which ones turn a week of work into an afternoon, and building them into how a team works.

I prototype brand systems, websites and apps with Claude, connect AI straight to production tools like Unity and OrchestrateXR, and use generative image, video and 3D tools in everyday production.
