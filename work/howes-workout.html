Challenge

Give our family, Dad, Eden and Aron, one training plan we can follow together at home, with everyone working at their own level.

Solution

  • Five training days in a 4-week cycle, each one a 30-minute session: warm-up, skill, circuit, stretch
  • A built-in clock for AMRAP or for-time circuits, with quick presets
  • A level ladder for every skill and exercise, tracked for each person
  • A record board for the test day at the end of every fourth week
  • Everything stays on the phone, with backup export and restore

My role

  • Designed and built the app with Claude, and deployed it on Netlify
