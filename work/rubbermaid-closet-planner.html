Challenge

Rubbermaid wanted more engagement and online revenue from its closet planner. Research showed the old tool didn’t answer the basic questions: how much will it hold, and what will fit?

Solution

  • A drag-and-drop planner built around people’s belongings, not products.
  • Live pricing, a full parts list and a direct path to buy at Lowes.com.
  • Save, print and share designs, with built-in analytics to measure behavior.

My role

  • Developed user journeys
  • Designed product and user workflows
  • Designed the interface
  • Developed 3D assets, motion graphics and animations
  • Provided quality assurance

Highlights

Results

20%conversion rate on all shopping carts
HorizonInteractive Award winner
  • A major retail partner called the tool “10 years ahead of what anyone else in the industry was doing.”
  • Built-in analytics let the team measure behavior and respond quickly.
