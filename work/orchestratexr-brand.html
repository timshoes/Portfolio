Challenge

OrchestrateVR set out to make VR creation as simple as PowerPoint, so subject matter experts could build it themselves. Instructors and trainers wanted to turn their existing live simulations into VR and reach more learners, but most had never built anything in VR. The brand had to feel friendly, and still read as reliable and empowering.

Solution

  • Built OrchestrateVR as an extension of the AccessVR brand, not just its flagship product: “Empowered by AccessVR.” The fonts and AccessVR Orange are used deliberately to carry the parent identity.
  • A figure with arms raised inside the O: the instructor as the one in control.
  • Bright orange and white on charcoal: approachable, confident and easy to spot.
  • The brand carried into the product itself, from the editor to the player.

My role

  • Led brand strategy and brand identity
  • Designed the logo and carried the brand into the product interface

Today

OrchestrateVR has since become OrchestrateXR, adding AI avatars and other XR capabilities, and OrchestrateXR is now the company name, replacing AccessVR.
