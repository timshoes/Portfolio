Challenge

AccessVR needed a brand that made virtual reality feel human and approachable to educators and trainers: early adopters excited about immersive learning, but not technologists.

Solution

  • Built the brand from the ground up: strategy, positioning, logo, identity and voice.
  • The big idea, “Bringing humanity to extended reality,” positioned AccessVR as the company making immersive learning accessible to everyone.
  • Built the identity: a mark made of rising angles, purple for creativity, orange for opportunity, set in Gotham and Merriweather.
  • Wrote the style guide and brand book, then carried the system across every touchpoint: business cards, the website, event graphics and a 10×30 booth at IMSH 2023.

  • Led brand strategy and brand identity
  • Developed the brand position, logo, tagline, voice and brand guidelines
