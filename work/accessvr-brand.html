Challenge

AccessVR needed a brand that made virtual reality feel human and approachable to educators and trainers: early adopters excited about immersive learning, but not technologists.

Solution

  • Positioned AccessVR as the company making immersive learning accessible to everyone, with the tagline “Bringing humanity to virtual reality.”
  • Built the identity: a mark made of rising angles, purple for creativity, orange for opportunity, set in Gotham and Merriweather.
  • Wrote the style guide and brand book that carried the system across business cards, the website and event graphics.
  • Took the brand to IMSH 2023: a 10×30 booth with back-lit purple towers, demo stations and roll-up banners.
